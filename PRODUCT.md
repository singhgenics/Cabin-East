## Users

- Holidaymakers and residents in South Goa (Cavelossim, Colva, Majorda, Varca) deciding where to eat and drink tonight, usually on a phone, often from the beach or a villa.
- Friends of the owners and early followers watching the place come together before it opens.

## Product Purpose

The public website for Cabin East, a small Pan-Asian pub that is **opening soon** in Cavelossim, South Goa. It tells people what the place is, shows the food and the drinks, and where to find it. Success: a visitor wants to come on opening week and remembers the name.

## Positioning

**The moat (owner, 2026-10-21): dim sum, bao, craft beer and craft cocktails.** The site leads with them, in that order: hero pitch, a four-cell moat strip, a Steam chapter (dim sum, bao), a Draught chapter (craft beer, craft cocktails), then the story and the wok. Wok stays a major pillar but no longer opens the page.

Indo-Chinese from the wok, dim sum and bao from the steamer, and craft beer from Goa's own small breweries on tap, in one small, warm room with a beer garden a short walk from the Arabian Sea. Tagline: WOK | STEAM | DRAUGHT. Local ingredients carry real stories (chouriço siu mai, kokum gose, Paul John single malt from Cuncolim, courtyard tulsi).

## Operating Context

- 28 covers: 18 inside, 10 in the beer garden.
- Hours (planned): 12 noon to 11 pm, closed Monday. Happy hour 12 noon to 6 pm (owner, 2026-10-20; menus still say 4-8 until updated). Lunch "Bowl game" noon to 4 pm.
- Location: Cavelossim, South Goa. The street address is deliberately left off the site for now (owner, 2026-10-29).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Story facts to keep hedged (sources differ): Atchew (Yang Dazhao) arrived in the 1770s (not 1778) and the village is Achipur; Tiretta Bazaar is the first Chinatown, Tangra the second (tanneries); Calcutta's cabins were curtained wooden booths; Chicken Manchurian is credited to Nelson Wang at the Cricket Club of India, Mumbai, 1975; chilli chicken's origin is one cook's recollection.

- No bookings, no ordering, no sign-up: the site is informational while the pub is pre-launch. Walk-ins only once open.
- No phone number, email or social handles exist yet. Do not invent them; leave space to add later.
- Opening date is not set. Say "opening soon", never a date.
- Menu and prices are the live working menu (source: Dream/food.html and drinks.html); they may still change.

## Product Principles

1. Warm before posh: it is a neighbourhood pub, not a fine-dining room.
2. Show the food and the pour; let photos sell.
3. Tell the story truthfully. The site's story section (owner request, 2026-10-21) tells how Indo-Chinese food was born in Calcutta and reached Goa, and is the one place that names Calcutta, Tiretta Bazaar, Tangra and the cabins. Everywhere else (menu, brand and marketing copy) the earlier rule holds: never mention Kolkata, Tangra or City of Joy (owner, 2026-10-20). Story pictures are labelled illustrations; history claims are hedged to what the sources support.
4. Never promise what doesn't exist yet (dates, bookings, contacts).
