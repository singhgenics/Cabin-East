## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step at runtime. Own public repo `singhgenics/CabinEast`, served by GitHub Pages at singhgenics.github.io/CabinEast. (User's choice, 2026-10-20.)

## Users

- Holidaymakers and residents in South Goa (Betalbatim, Colva, Majorda, Varca) deciding where to eat and drink tonight, usually on a phone, often from the beach or a villa.
- Friends of the owners and early followers watching the place come together before it opens.

## Product Purpose

The public website for Cabin East, a small Pan-Asian pub that is **opening soon** in Betalbatim, South Goa. It tells people what the place is, shows the food and the drinks, and where to find it. Success: a visitor wants to come on opening week and remembers the name.

## Positioning

Indo-Chinese from the wok, dim sum and bao from the steamer, and craft beer from Goa's own small breweries on tap, in one small, warm room with a beer garden a short walk from the Arabian Sea. Tagline: WOK | STEAM | DRAUGHT. Local ingredients carry real stories (chouriço siu mai, kokum gose, Paul John single malt from Cuncolim, courtyard tulsi).

## Operating Context

- 28 covers: 18 inside, 10 in the beer garden.
- Hours (planned): 12 noon to 11 pm, closed Monday. Happy hour 12 noon to 6 pm (owner, 2026-10-20; menus still say 4-8 until updated). Lunch "Bowl game" noon to 4 pm.
- Address: Shop 4, Tulip Residency, Gandaulim, Betalbatim, South Goa.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No bookings, no ordering, no sign-up: the site is informational while the pub is pre-launch. Walk-ins only once open.
- No phone number, email or social handles exist yet. Do not invent them; leave space to add later.
- Opening date is not set. Say "opening soon", never a date.
- Menu and prices are the live working menu (source: Dream/food.html and drinks.html); they may still change.

## Brand Commitments

- Name **Cabin East**, tagline **WOK | STEAM | DRAUGHT**, always set under the logo.
- Master logo: the pint-stack (glass holding foam, steamer with dumpling, flame and wok) with Alfa Slab One "CABIN EAST". Assets in Dream/CabinEast/brand-kit/logo.
- Tangra Red palette: red #B5321C, cream #F2E8D2, ink #1C1714, amber #E49B1D, jade #2F6F57. Alfa Slab One display, IBM Plex Sans text, IBM Plex Mono labels.
- Dish names are playful puns (Cabin Fever, Wonton Kenobi, Gose Bumps); keep them exactly.
- Inspiration given by the owner: craft-pub WordPress themes with cream paper, heavy slab headings, dark bands, photo mosaics (Website/reference). Premium but not intimidating.

## Evidence on Hand

- Plated dish and drink photos (Google Flow, 1200×896): Dream/Menu-repo/img/plating.
- No reviews, press, awards or customer quotes. Never fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Warm before posh: it is a neighbourhood pub, not a fine-dining room.
2. Show the food and the pour; let photos sell.
3. Tell the local story truthfully: Goa ingredients and taps. Never mention Kolkata, Tangra or City of Joy (owner, 2026-10-20).
4. Never promise what doesn't exist yet (dates, bookings, contacts).
